CHANGE MANAGEMENT — PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN Weekly Activity Plan
and Feedback Guide

HOW TO USE THIS TEXT - Each workstream includes its objective, activity
details, and Week 1–Week 8 feedback prompts. - Copy the relevant text
into the corresponding Excel cell. Week labels are explicit to make each
weekly entry easy to identify. - Week 1 focuses on independent
understanding, solution proposal and planning. The employee proposes the
detailed sequence and milestones for Weeks 2–8; the manager reviews and
agrees the plan. - Weeks 2–8 are assessed against the mutually agreed
Week 1 plan, not against a predetermined feature-by-week sequence.

============================================================================== 1.
Technical Excellence — Scalable Design, Architecture & Innovation
==============================================================================

OBJECTIVE Demonstrate the ability to independently design and deliver
scalable, extensible and configuration-driven solutions that address
current business needs while anticipating future requirements, improving
engineering efficiency and minimizing rework.

ACTIVITY DETAILS - Design and implement a configurable Change Management
and Impact Analysis solution supporting version comparison, change
identification, downstream impact analysis, review, approval and
controlled publication. - Integrate relevant services to identify
impacts on projects, technology combinations, assessments and discovery
outcomes. - Develop reusable, configuration-driven capabilities that can
accommodate new workflows, approval rules, notifications and impact
scenarios with minimal code changes. - Ensure controlled versioning and
publication so downstream applications consume the correct approved and
published version. - Document and present architecture, design
alternatives, scalability considerations and technical trade-offs. -
Identify engineering improvements that reduce duplication, simplify
enhancements and improve reusability. - AI-assisted engineering:
demonstrate at least two practical use cases; document and share at
least two reusable practices with the team; demonstrate adoption or
piloting of at least one practice by another developer where feasible.

WEEK 1 FEEDBACK Assess whether the employee independently identifies the
end-to-end technical scope, proposes component boundaries and API
contracts, considers extensibility/configurability, and presents design
options and trade-offs. Confirm the plan is reviewed and agreed; initial
technical work should begin where practical.

WEEK 2 FEEDBACK Assess technical quality of the first milestones in the
approved plan. Review API/design consistency, modularity, code quality
and whether early implementation supports the proposed architecture.
Record review findings and rework attributable to avoidable design gaps.

WEEK 3 FEEDBACK Assess whether the evolving solution remains consistent
and extensible as agreed components progress. Review API contracts,
component boundaries, design decisions and handling of new information
or feedback.

WEEK 4 FEEDBACK Assess technical consistency across components being
integrated under the approved plan. Review whether workflows, business
rules and impact types use appropriate reusable/configurable structures
where justified.

WEEK 5 FEEDBACK Assess whether stakeholder feedback is incorporated
without unnecessary structural redesign. Review maintainability, code
review findings and any technical debt or rework arising from design
gaps.

WEEK 6 FEEDBACK Assess technical decisions relating to publication,
version control, monitoring and operational needs as applicable to the
approved plan. Review configuration and coupling concerns.

WEEK 7 FEEDBACK Assess whether technical gaps, defects and
maintainability concerns identified during stabilization are addressed.
Review consistency and readiness of technical documentation.

WEEK 8 FEEDBACK Assess the final solution against agreed architecture
and extensibility expectations. Review technical documentation and
evidence of engineering improvements, including the two AI-assisted use
cases, shared practices and adoption/pilot evidence where feasible.

==============================================================================
2. Quality & Operational Resilience — Change Management
==============================================================================

OBJECTIVE Improve the stability, correctness and resilience of Change
Management through automated testing, regression protection, internal
reconciliation, monitoring and failure handling, reducing recurring
defects, avoidable rework and manual retesting.

ACTIVITY DETAILS - Establish automated unit, integration, regression and
end-to-end tests covering critical business rules, edge cases, service
integrations and previously identified defects. - Integrate automated
tests into the delivery pipeline with appropriate quality gates to
prevent known regressions from being released. - Identify recurring
defects and address root causes, protecting corrective changes with
regression tests. - Implement validation and reconciliation to detect
missing, incomplete, inconsistent or unsuccessfully processed
information within Change Management processing and publication. -
Establish proactive monitoring and actionable alerts for processing
failures, internal reconciliation mismatches, missing information and
failed service integrations. - Implement error handling, traceability
and recovery mechanisms to maintain data integrity and prevent silent
failures or unintended downstream changes. - Validate failure scenarios,
including dependency failures, failed impact analysis, workflow failures
and publication failures. - Track defect recurrence, escaped defects,
manual regression effort and avoidable rework against an agreed
baseline.

WEEK 1 FEEDBACK Assess whether the employee’s proposed plan includes
task breakdown, estimates, dependencies, acceptance criteria, testing,
failure scenarios, monitoring and operational readiness. Confirm a
realistic plan for the remaining seven weeks is agreed and risks and
assumptions are recorded.

WEEK 2 FEEDBACK Assess whether tests are developed alongside the first
agreed implementation, progress is tracked against commitments, and
defects/dependencies are raised early. Review tests for relevant
business rules and failure scenarios in the work delivered.

WEEK 3 FEEDBACK Assess whether testing and validation keep pace with
implementation. Review API/component tests, edge cases, defect handling
and whether identified issues are protected by regression tests.

WEEK 4 FEEDBACK Assess quality across the agreed workflow and
integration milestones. Check relevant failure handling, state
transitions, and automated coverage for the functionality delivered.

WEEK 5 FEEDBACK Assess integration and end-to-end test coverage for the
current agreed scope. Review whether feedback-driven changes are tested
and whether defects are root-caused rather than patched narrowly.

WEEK 6 FEEDBACK Assess implementation and validation of agreed
publication controls, processing-status visibility, monitoring and
actionable alerts. Review evidence for failure scenarios.

WEEK 7 FEEDBACK Assess agreed internal reconciliation, recovery,
regression and stabilization activities. Review recurring defects,
root-cause actions and operational gaps.

WEEK 8 FEEDBACK Assess final acceptance and regression evidence, defect
status, monitoring/alert validation, recovery demonstrations and
handover readiness. Compare defect recurrence, escaped defects, manual
regression effort and avoidable rework with the agreed baseline where
measurable.

==============================================================================
3. Operating Effectiveness — Solution Ownership, Stakeholder Influence &
Delivery Agility
==============================================================================

OBJECTIVE Demonstrate proactive ownership of requirements and delivery
by independently analysing business needs, proposing effective
solutions, coordinating with stakeholders, executing rapidly and
incorporating feedback through iterative demonstrations, while improving
user experience and accommodating evolving requirements efficiently.

ACTIVITY DETAILS - Independently analyse business requirements, user
needs, workflows and dependencies, and develop a solution proposal
before implementation. - Present recommended solutions, alternatives,
trade-offs and expected user/business benefits. - Proactively coordinate
with Product Owners, users and technical stakeholders to align on
outcomes, obtain feedback and drive decisions/actions to closure. -
Break development into manageable increments and deliver early working
implementations or demonstrations. - Incorporate agreed feedback through
short iterations, minimizing late changes, avoidable rework and
delays. - Design workflows, business rules and user-facing capabilities
to be configurable where appropriate. - Identify and propose
improvements to user experience, workflow efficiency and business
functionality beyond immediate requirements. - Communicate progress,
dependencies, risks and blockers proactively, with recommended
resolutions and accountability for milestones.

WEEK 1 FEEDBACK Assess whether the employee leads requirement
clarification with the PO/stakeholders, identifies workflow and
publication needs, proposes a solution and independently creates the
delivery breakdown for Weeks 2–8. Review the proposal, trade-off
rationale, stakeholder alignment and response to feedback during plan
review.

WEEK 2 FEEDBACK Assess ownership of initial commitments, proactive
communication, progress demonstration at an appropriate checkpoint and
follow-through on stakeholder decisions. Check that feedback is captured
with actions and that risks/blockers are raised with proposed
resolutions.

WEEK 3 FEEDBACK Assess continued stakeholder engagement and
follow-through on agreed feedback. Review whether decisions and
dependencies are actively managed and progress is communicated against
the approved plan.

WEEK 4 FEEDBACK Assess how effectively the employee coordinates
stakeholders and technical dependencies, demonstrates working increments
as appropriate, and incorporates agreed feedback. Review prioritization
and decision-making.

WEEK 5 FEEDBACK Assess whether the employee manages integration and
feedback dependencies proactively, communicates risks early and avoids
unplanned changes without alignment.

WEEK 6 FEEDBACK Assess proactive management of delivery and operational
dependencies, timely risk communication and appropriate replanning when
assumptions or requirements change.

WEEK 7 FEEDBACK Assess follow-through on stabilization issues, defect
resolution, residual risks and stakeholder actions. Review communication
of status and any revised commitments.

WEEK 8 FEEDBACK Assess end-to-end ownership, completion or disposition
of feedback actions, communication of residual risks and
release/handover readiness. Review planned-versus-actual delivery,
lessons learned and evidence of independent problem-solving throughout
the PIP.

==============================================================================
ASSESSMENT GUIDANCE
==============================================================================

How to use the weekly columns

Week 1 assesses independent requirements understanding, solution
proposal and delivery planning. The employee proposes the detailed
milestones and task sequence for Weeks 2–8; the manager reviews and
agrees the plan.

Weeks 2–8 are assessed individually against the milestones in the
mutually agreed Week 1 plan. Weekly feedback should reflect actual
progress and the three workstreams, not a prescribed implementation
sequence.

Week 8 assesses final outcomes, evidence, handover and sustained
performance across the three workstreams.

Suggested weekly assessment: Met / Partially Met / Not Met / Not
Applicable. Record actual evidence, gaps, impact, corrective action,
owner and due date in the feedback cells or in a linked review record.

Scope: Change Management only. Coverage delivery and reconciliation
within downstream consumer systems are excluded. Publication through the
agreed interface and monitoring of publication/processing outcomes
remain in scope.
